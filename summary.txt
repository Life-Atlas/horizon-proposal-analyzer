WINNIIO proposes The Plant That Reasons for ADNOC Challenges 1414 and 1342: a governed planning and turnaround twin delivering the briefs' named outcomes — Maintenance Work Duration and Shutdown Duration Optimizers with norms mined from ADNOC's own completion history; a criticality-tiered Proactive Indicator alerting before compliance dates are breached, inside mobilisation lead times; accurate PM forecasting with manpower from last completion-order history; Delay and Work Delay Forecasters by progress comparison; a Schedule Shortfalls Optimizer with simulated alternate solutions; Area Campaign bundling scenarios; a Turnaround Criticality Calculator; and automatic ingestion of P6, SAP, OSI PI, Microsoft and PDF sources into one ontology owned by ADNOC in open formats, generating traffic lights, Gantt, S-curves and daily reports from a single model. Capability climbs a five-rung ladder (answers, shows, tells, prescribes, acts): rungs 1-2 delivered in the reference engagement, rung 3 demonstrated as risk-tiered dashboard flagging, rung 4 partially evidenced (gap quantification), rung 5 roadmap under ADNOC governance — all autonomy bounded, auditable, overridable. Delivery follows the published SMILE methodology (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21268264), aligned to the Digital Twin Consortium's Capabilities Periodic Table and Composability Framework. Because our proof is adjacent-industry (a delivered production-planning twin at a European steel manufacturer, not oil and gas), the entry is de-risked: a 12-week fixed-price pilot back-tested on one completed ADNOC turnaround plus one live maintenance-plan export, with a contractual honest-negative clause and full artefact handover in open formats regardless of continuation. Fully on-premise; no data leaves ADNOC's perimeter. The pilot is the justification.
